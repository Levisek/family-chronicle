# The Černýs of Prague and Brno

An invented chronicle for the example data. The people, places and sources
are all made up; the markers are real and drive the map and the person cards.

## Prague printers

<!-- place: Prague -->
<!-- person: I37; I38 -->
František Černý (1872-1938) kept a small print shop in Prague and married
Ludmila Horáková, who was born before 1880 in the same city.[^1] Of their
life together we know little beyond the shop itself.

<!-- person: I19 -->
Their son Václav, born in 1900, learned the trade and worked as a printer by
1925. Later he became a union representative and spoke for the printers of his
street at meetings in the city.[^2] He died in Prague in 1968.

## A marriage across Moravia

<!-- place: Brno; Prague -->
<!-- person: I20; I39; I40 -->
Václav met Anežka Dvořáková in Brno. She was born there in 1905, the
daughter of Karel Dvořák and Alžběta Procházková, and they married in Brno in
1928.[^3] The couple then settled in Prague. Anežka most likely kept the
household accounts for the print shop, since her handwriting appears in the
surviving ledger, but this is only an impression from a few pages.

## The next generation

<!-- place: Prague -->
<!-- person: I21; I22 -->
Their son Josef (1930) married Jitka Svobodová of Brno in 1955 and remained in
Prague. Josef died in 1999, Jitka in 2015.[^4]

<!-- person: I6 -->
Their daughter Helena, born in Prague in 1937, left for Linz after her marriage
to Karl Huber in 1957; see the Huber chronicle for the rest of her life. She
died in Linz in 2010.

<!-- person: I23 -->
Josef's son Pavel (1960) was born in Prague. <!-- web:hide -->His grandson, a
child born in 2018, now lives in Brno.<!-- /web:hide --><!-- web:text: Pavel's descendants live in Brno. -->

## What we don't know

<!-- person: I38 -->
- Where Ludmila Horáková was born and the name of her family's village.[^1]
- Whether the print shop survived the war or was closed earlier.
- What became of the ledger after 1960.
