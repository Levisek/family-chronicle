The Hubers of Vienna and Linz

This is an invented chronicle that ships with the example data. It shows the markers the site generator understands; every person, place and source in it is made up.

Vienna, the old home

The family story begins with Johann Huber (1848-1915), a cartwright who worked in a yard near the Danube canal in Vienna. In 1875 he married Barbara Maier (1850-1921), the daughter of a neighbouring baker.1 They had two sons who reached adulthood, Josef (1878) and Anton (1880).

Josef Huber took over the yard and married Rosa Bruckner in 1903. Rosa was probably born around 1882, but no baptism entry has turned up, so the date comes from her marriage record only.2 Josef died in 1949 and Rosa a year later.

Anton, the younger brother, left the trade and became a clerk at a railway office. His only son Rudolf (1910) later settled in Linz, where he died in 1980.3

A tailor in the family

Josef's son Franz Huber was born in Vienna in 1905. By 1930 he worked as a tailor in the city.4 In 1929 he married Theresia Berger, who had been born in Linz in 1908, and the couple moved between the two towns for several years before settling in Linz for good. Franz died in 1971; Theresia outlived him by nine years.

Their daughter Hilde (1930) married Otto Maier in 1953, but the marriage did not last and ended in divorce in 1965. Their son Peter, born in Linz in 1956, kept the Maier name.

Karl and the move to Linz

Karl Huber, born in Vienna in 1934, married Helena Černá in Linz in 1957. Her family came from Prague and Brno; their own story is told in the Černý chronicle.5 Karl worked in a printing house and, according to family memory, spent most evenings repairing clocks.

Karl and Helena had two sons, Gerhard (1959) and Thomas (1961), who each grew up in Linz. Thomas died there in 2015. Thomas's son Daniel, born on 12 March 1990, still keeps the clock collection in his flat.

What we don't know

  • Where Rosa Bruckner was born and who her parents were.2
  • Whether Anton Huber's railway office was in Vienna or in Linz.
  • Why Franz and Theresia moved so often between 1929 and 1935.
